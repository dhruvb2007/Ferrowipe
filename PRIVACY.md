# Privacy Policy for Ferrowipe

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Publisher:** Karshanbhai Haribhai Technologies (Dhruv Prajapati)  
**Application:** Ferrowipe (Windows Desktop Application)

---

## 1. Overview

Ferrowipe is built with a strict **privacy-first, offline-only** architecture. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or file data.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Ferrowipe operates entirely locally on your Windows device. Specifically, Ferrowipe does **not** collect or send:

* Personal information (name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or IP address)
* File names, folder paths, file contents, or drive inventories
* Lists of installed applications, browser profiles, or cache contents
* Telemetry, diagnostic logs, analytics, or advertising identifiers

Ferrowipe requires **no user account** and contains **no ads or third-party tracking SDKs**.

---

## 3. Local Data Processed on Your Device

To provide disk optimization, junk cleaning, space analysis, and app storage inspection, Ferrowipe reads local file system metadata, Recycle Bin statistics, and read-only Windows Uninstall registry entries **strictly on your device**:

* **Local Disk Index & Preferences:** Ferrowipe stores lightweight UI preferences (such as theme and sidebar state) and local disk index snapshots inside your user profile (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Ferrowipe`) solely to speed up subsequent launches. This data never leaves your PC and is automatically removed when you uninstall or clear local app data.
* **User-Initiated Cleaning & Deletion:** Files and caches are only removed when you explicitly preview and confirm a cleanup or deletion action. Space Analyzer deletions move items to the Windows Recycle Bin by default unless you explicitly select permanent deletion.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Ferrowipe does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or cloud storage services. If you download Ferrowipe through the Microsoft Store or GitHub, those platforms handle downloads and updates under their own respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because Ferrowipe does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If Ferrowipe ever changes how it handles data in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated at this location with a new effective date prior to release.
